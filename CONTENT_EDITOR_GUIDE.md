# Legacy Beacon content editor guide

## Sign in

Open Pages CMS and sign in with the GitHub account authorised for the Legacy Beacon repository. Use two-factor authentication on the account.

## Edit or create an article

1. Open **Resources** and choose an article, or select **New**.
2. Update the title, summary, category, content, image, and SEO fields.
3. Keep **Publication status** set to **draft** while the article is being reviewed.
4. Change it to **published** only after content and compliance approval.
5. Save. Pages CMS creates a GitHub commit and Cloudflare Pages rebuilds the site.

Use a short, descriptive filename/slug. Provide a unique SEO title and meta description. Preview and proofread every article before publishing.

## Site information

Use **Global settings** to update company contact details and social links. Use **Services** to edit service descriptions and **Frequently asked questions** to update answers. Do not change URL slugs without developer help because existing links and redirects may depend on them.

## Do not change

Do not edit components, layouts, navigation code, styles, deployment files, environment variables, or the Pages CMS configuration without developer assistance. Do not publish licensing, legal, insurance-product, tax, return, or guarantee language until it has been approved by Legacy Beacon's responsible reviewer.
