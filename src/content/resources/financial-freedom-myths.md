<h3><strong>Myth #1: “I Need to Earn More Before I Can Save.”</strong></h3>
<p>If that were true, even people with high incomes would have no debt — but that’s not the case.</p>
<p>
The real issue isn’t income; it’s <em>intention</em>.</p>
<p>
Financial freedom starts with managing what you already have.</p>
<p>
Even small, consistent savings (₦5,000 or $20 at a time) can grow into a financial safety net over time.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>💬 “Don’t wait for more to do better; start with what’s in your hands.”</p>
</blockquote>
<h3><strong>Myth #2: “Financial Planning Is Only for the Wealthy.”</strong></h3>
<p>This is one of the biggest misconceptions we see.</p>
<p>
In reality, the <strong>wealthy are wealthy because they plan</strong>.</p>
<p>
Financial planning is simply a roadmap — one that anyone can follow.</p>
<p>
With guidance, you can protect your income, reduce unnecessary spending, and make your money work for you.</p>
<p>At Legacy Beacon, we make financial planning accessible to everyone — no jargon, no intimidation, just practical steps.</p>
<h3><strong>Myth #3: “It’s Too Late for Me.”</strong></h3>
<p>Whether you’re 25 or 65, there’s always a next right step.</p>
<p>
Financial transformation doesn’t start with perfection; it starts with <strong>awareness and action</strong>.</p>
<p>
You can build a better future by starting where you are — right now.</p>
<p>Remember: your legacy doesn’t begin when you’re gone; it begins the moment you decide to live intentionally.</p>
